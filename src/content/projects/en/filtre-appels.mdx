## Screen calls without collecting them

FiltreAppels lets people define exact-number or prefix rules, then allow, silence, or block matching calls. The app works without an account, subscription, server, or Internet permission.

Incoming numbers are never logged or stored. Local statistics only contain aggregated actions and rule identifiers.

## A deterministic decision engine

Allow exceptions take priority over broader rules. The engine then compares exact numbers before selecting the longest blocking or silencing prefix. A built-in tester explains the decision without changing statistics.

Normalization supports French formats and several overseas territories. A versioned list of ARCEP ranges can be explicitly enabled by the user.

## A protected critical path

The Telecom service performs no disk or network access while making a decision. It reads an immutable compiled in-memory snapshot and responds before statistics are updated asynchronously. Invalid input or unexpected failure allows the call by default.

The MVP still needs validation across more devices, manufacturers, and real-call scenarios before a wider release.
