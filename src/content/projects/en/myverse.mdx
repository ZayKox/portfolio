## One cross-media cultural profile

MyVerse starts from a simple observation: movies, TV series, games, anime, manga, and books are usually tracked across separate services. The product builds a canonical catalog that can bring those fields together without erasing what makes each one different.

The personal library remains private. Public profiles, reviews, and lists live in a separate layer, while recommendations provide readable reasons instead of an opaque score.

## A full-stack foundation

The project is a modular monolith built with Next.js and strict TypeScript. PostgreSQL and Prisma support the catalog, personal states, and social layer. Data from TMDB, IGDB, AniList, Open Library, and Google Books is validated and normalized before import.

Imports are designed to be idempotent and safe under concurrency. The model also keeps media-specific concepts separate: duration, episodes, chapters, and pages are not reduced to one generic measurement.

## Current validation

The repository includes unit, component, PostgreSQL, and Playwright tests, alongside security, privacy, and release checks. These are local engineering and validation signals, not yet metrics from a public production service.

The final case study will add the personal context, trade-offs, false starts, tester feedback, and a complete visual walkthrough.
